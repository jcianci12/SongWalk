What is SongWalk?

A self-hosted music dropbox — create libraries, share them with a link, and listen together in real time.

🎵

Listen Together

Real-time sync playback across devices. Scan a QR code, join a session — play, pause, seek, and skip mirror instantly. No host, whoever presses first wins.

📱

PWA + Offline

Install to home screen on iOS & Android. Tap Offline to cache all tracks via service worker. Full playback works without internet.

🔗

Magic Link Auth

Enter your email, get a one-click link. No passwords, no accounts — just you and your music.

🎨

Retro WMP UI

Windows Media Player-inspired design with pixel-art spinning CD, drag-to-reorder tracks and albums, inline editing, and star ratings.

⬇️

Import from Anywhere

Drag-and-drop audio files, paste YouTube URLs, or pull from Spotify — all into any shared library. MusicBrainz auto-fills missing tags.

🔒

Share-First, Private

Each library gets a UUID-backed URL. Share it — anyone with the link can listen and upload. The landing page never leaks library IDs.
